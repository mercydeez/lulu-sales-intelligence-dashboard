# Visual Language

## 1. Creative Direction

Retail IQ should feel like a premium enterprise operating system.

Reference qualities:

- Apple in finish and restraint
- Stripe in clarity and technical confidence
- Sprinto in trust framing and governance seriousness

The mood is calm, premium, intelligent, and operational.

## 2. Color Direction

### 2.1 Foundation palette

Recommended working values:

- `canvas`: `#F7F4EF`
- `surface`: `#FFFFFF`
- `surface-elevated`: `#FCFBF9`
- `surface-tint`: `#F3F8F7`
- `line`: `#E6DED4`
- `ink`: `#171717`
- `ink-soft`: `#48515A`
- `ink-muted`: `#6F7A84`

### 2.2 Brand palette

- `brand-teal-700`: `#0F766E`
- `brand-teal-600`: `#14867D`
- `brand-teal-100`: `#D7F1ED`
- `brand-teal-50`: `#EEF9F7`

### 2.3 Semantic palette

- success: emerald-led
- warning: amber-led
- danger: coral-led
- info: blue-led
- AI: violet-led, never primary brand

Recommended anchors:

- `success`: `#0F9F6E`
- `warning`: `#B7791F`
- `danger`: `#D95F4A`
- `info`: `#2563EB`
- `ai`: `#6E59F6`

## 3. Color Usage Rules

- teal is the primary brand and action color
- violet is reserved for AI provenance and assistive moments
- coral is reserved for risk, destructive actions, or negative outcomes
- charts must not use coral as the default hero series
- trust and admin pages should rely more on neutrals than accent colors

## 4. Typography

### 4.1 Type stack

- UI and display: `Manrope`
- Arabic companion: `IBM Plex Sans Arabic` or `Noto Sans Arabic`
- mono: `IBM Plex Mono`

### 4.2 Tone

- sentence case over title case or all caps
- short, direct headings
- concise body copy
- AI copy should sound grounded, not theatrical

### 4.3 Numeric treatment

- use tabular numerals
- align numbers carefully in tables and KPI strips
- keep the unit or currency close to the number

## 5. Shape And Surface

### 5.1 Radius

- controls: 12-16px
- standard cards: 20px
- focal modules: 24px
- pills: full radius

### 5.2 Depth

Use soft, broad shadows.

Avoid:

- heavy floating card stacks
- glossy or glassy surfaces used everywhere
- hard black shadows

### 5.3 Surface treatment

- standard cards are matte and quiet
- hero moments may use subtle mesh gradients
- AI and trust areas may use low-chroma tinted backgrounds
- use glass effects only in limited overlay moments

## 6. Layout Mood

- spacious first impression
- tighter density once the user enters table or workflow-heavy areas
- strong grouping rather than many dividers
- generous whitespace around hero and onboarding surfaces

## 7. Charts And Data Viz

Rules:

- charts must be annotation-led and readable at a glance
- the most important series gets teal when appropriate
- risk movement may use coral
- legends should be minimal and direct labels preferred when possible
- avoid novelty charts that obscure comparison

## 8. Motion Language

Use motion to signal hierarchy and state change.

Use:

- fade and slight rise on page entry
- subtle hover lift
- drawer slide and fade
- live-update pulse only when useful

Avoid:

- bouncing cards
- ambient motion loops across the whole page
- delayed animation that blocks reading

Reduced motion must preserve hierarchy without relying on animation.

## 9. Iconography And Illustration

- use one outline icon family for the full product
- icons support labels rather than replacing them
- no stock photography inside core app flows
- onboarding and empty states may use restrained diagrams or abstract illustrations

## 10. Copy Feel

The product voice should feel:

- calm
- exact
- useful
- enterprise-ready

Avoid:

- hype language
- exclamation-heavy copy
- casual jokes in workflow-critical surfaces

## 11. Accessibility Requirements

- all text and controls must meet WCAG AA contrast
- charts need labels, notes, or tooltips beyond color
- destructive and success states require icon or text pairing
- layouts must remain coherent in RTL and Arabic-localized contexts

## 12. Anti-Patterns

Do not generate or design:

- dark-mode-first dashboards
- purple-primary brand systems
- neon trading-terminal aesthetics
- decorative gradients everywhere
- giant metric walls with no action path
