# Retail IQ — UAE go-to-market (30/60/90)

> Companion to `PRODUCT_STRATEGY.md` and `SAAS_PATH.md`. This is the commercial play
> now that the product carries the full acquire→activate→prove→close→bill→retain→
> re-price motion in-product.

## The wedge (commit to it)
Sell **one measurable outcome**, not "retail intelligence":
> *"We cut your dead-stock and stockouts on fresh/perishables and prove the saving
> in dirhams — every recommendation is human-approved."*

- **ICP:** 5–50 store regional **grocery + pharmacy** chains in the UAE. Too big for
  spreadsheets, too small for SAP/Oracle Retail, bleeding cash on perishables.
- **The number that closes:** the `/value` headline — cash to release (dead-stock) +
  recover (stockout loss), in AED, from the customer's own imported data. Export the
  CFO report for their finance team.

## The product motion (all built, in-app)
1. **Show** — `/value` on the demo tenant (full AED story).
2. **Prove** — prospect imports their CSV at `/imports` (sample CSVs provided) → their number appears.
3. **De-risk** — `/trust` page + `SECURITY_ONE_PAGER.md` for the IT/security review.
4. **Close** — export the CFO ROI report (`/value` → Report).
5. **Bill** — manual invoice / PO / bank-transfer with VAT (printable Tax Invoice) + e-invoicing readiness.
6. **Retain** — the measure loop proves recommendations worked; action drafting turns insight into approved action.
7. **Re-price** — `/outcome-pricing`: bill a share of operator-confirmed savings with a guarantee.

## 30 / 60 / 90
- **30 days** — finish 3–5 warm UAE customer-discovery calls; run the self-contained
  pilot demo (Show→Prove→De-risk) end-to-end; line up 2–3 paid pilots (AED 15–25k, 6–8 wks).
- **60 days** — pilots live on real data; instrument time-to-first-value and AED-found
  obsessively (the onboarding checklist + `/value` make both visible).
- **90 days** — first pilot → paid conversion + a written case study ("AED X released,
  Y% stockout cut, measured"). Use it to open the next 10 conversations.

## Risks that kill the company (and the mitigation already in place)
- **No real-data path → no pilot.** Mitigated: CSV import + sample CSVs shipped.
- **Forecast underwhelms on messy data.** Lead with diagnostics + *measured* outcomes
  (deterministic, defensible), not prediction magic.
- **Security review fails.** `/trust` + security one-pager + RLS isolation proof.
- **Selling breadth not the wedge.** Discipline: one category, one chain, one proven number.
