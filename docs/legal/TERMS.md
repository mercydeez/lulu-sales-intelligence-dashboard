## 1. Nature of the Service

**Retail IQ** is provided for a **pilot/evaluation**. Forecasts, findings, and AI
answers are **decision-support outputs**, not guarantees. The pilot operates on
**synthetic or sample data** unless the tenant loads its own data. The Service is
**not** a point-of-sale (POS) system, a system of record, or a financial-reporting
system, and must not be relied on as one.

## 2. Acceptable use

- Each tenant accesses only its own data via its own accounts and API keys.
- API keys are credentials; keep them secret and rotate/expire them. The operator
  may revoke keys that appear compromised.
- No attempt to access another tenant's data, probe isolation controls, or
  overload the Service.

## 3. AI outputs

AI answers are grounded in the tenant's own data and carry an evidence block. They
may be incomplete or wrong; a human must review before acting. The Service takes
**no autonomous action** on a tenant's behalf.

## 4. Availability

The pilot is provided **as is**, without uptime guarantees. Recovery objectives
(RPO/RTO) are documented for planning, not as a contractual SLA, unless a separate
signed agreement says otherwise.

deletion runbook. Deletion requests are human-approved.
