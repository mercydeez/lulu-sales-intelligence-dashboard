Built as a new module on top of Retail IQ

Flywheel AI · · "the loop"

An end-to-end, closed-loop retention engine. It doesn't just predict who will churn — it decides whether an offer is incrementally worth it, checks the shelf, routes the budget for approval, goes live, and measures the real lift — then feeds that result back into the next decision. The governing rule: models propose; only deterministic, policy-checked services touch money or stock.

Model — proposes & explains (no side-effects)
Service — acts on money/stock, row-locked & audited
Money — Decimal / NUMERIC, Postgres is truth
  • 1DATA — synthetic customers, events & inventory (tenant-scoped)
  • 2INTELLIGENCE — churn + uplift: who's at risk & would an offer help?
  • 3DECISION — pick the action that maximizes incremental value
  • 4COMMERCIAL — inventory, procurement, budget & stacked approval
  • 5EXECUTION — go live, decide in real time, redeem safely
  • 6MEASUREMENT — true lift vs a control group → feeds stage 1

03

Decision — next-best-action

Service

A deterministic optimizer — containing no model — ranks typed actions by expected incremental value and stores every term plus the rejected alternatives, so each decision is reproducible from its inputs. Benefits multiply uplift, never churn probability: that is why a high risk score alone can never trigger a discount.
